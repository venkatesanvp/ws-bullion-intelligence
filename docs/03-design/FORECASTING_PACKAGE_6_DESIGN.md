## Scoped decisions

New pure performance module consumes existing ExperimentEvidence and versioned descriptors, not provider data acquisition. A model-independent immutable contract records instrument/currency/basis, horizon, mode, model/version/configuration, dataset/experiment IDs, period, metrics, own eligible origins versus scored comparison origins, pending/excluded counts and per-card overlap/disjoint counts. When B01+B02 are requested, both point scorecards/errors/regime summaries use only the intersection of matured compatible instrument/origin/target/reference/actual cases; disclose dropped unmatched cases. B03 is separate volatility-proxy evidence. Single point-model requests remain own-sample summaries and cannot manufacture a paired model comparison. Reject incompatible mode/dataset/window/horizon/contracts before external card comparison. No leaderboards, winners or significance claims.

Signed price error=forecast-actual; absolute error=abs(signed); percentage error=100*(forecast-actual)/actual; return error=predicted_simple_return-(actual/origin-1). Threshold1e-8 guards normalized denominators: zero or abs(price)<=threshold -> null normalized field with reason, never epsilon replacement. Existing primary metric equations remain unchanged and use their existing positive Price contract. B03 signed error=forecast horizon sigma-realized-volatility proxy, with no directional/price/percentage metric. All authoritative math Decimal34; wire12 fixed strings. Rolling summaries are trailing scored origins ordered by origin, default10/configurable2..30, partial-window counts explicit. They are post-outcome diagnostics at actual assessment, with latest outcome-availability stamp; they cannot be historical origin inputs.

Error distribution uses signed return-error fractions for point models and separate volatility-proxy-error fractions for B03. Fixed seven bins: x<-.05;[-.05,-.01);[-.01,0);exact0;(0,.01];(.01,.05];x>.05. Quantiles p10/median/p90 nearest-rank, no interpolated probability or normality assumption. Excluded normalized errors counted. No price/volatility unit mixing. Descriptive summaries are not calibrated distributions.

Disjoint windows mean maximum non-overlapping realized target intervals (origin,target], using reviewed session indices and greedy interval scheduling. Overlap pairs and eligible/scored/requested counts remain separate. Effective statistically independent evidence is null/NOT_ESTIMATED: no autocorrelation, HAC, bootstrap, hypothesis test or effective-sample-size model is claimed. Every card warns that disjointness does not prove independence; small support and conditional-mode limitations remain visible.

Optional regimes, off by default: last21 completed contiguous closes from the exact already-built cutoff snapshot. Trend efficiency=abs(last-first)/sum(abs(close changes)); flat denominator ->0. TRENDING iff efficiency>=.35 AND abs(20-session simple return)>=.02, else SIDEWAYS. Volatility=sample sigma of20simple returns; HIGH iff sigma>=.015, otherwise LOW. Labeling happens before outcome access; no global/full-series quantile or future-target data. Conditional regime labels retain the same unverified-vintage/availability assumptions as their forecasts. Report two separate axes, not mutually exclusive labels; regime-subgroup losses are descriptive and small-sample warned.
